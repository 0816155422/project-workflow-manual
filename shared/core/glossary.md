# Glossary

- **Shared memory:** Content intentionally available for cross-platform use.
- **Approved memory:** Shared content explicitly reviewed and approved by a human.
- **Private memory:** Personal/device-specific content that must remain outside the repository.
- **Platform memory:** Notes, prompts, or snapshots tailored to one AI product.
- **Snapshot:** Dated export of selected context at a point in time.
- **Prompt export:** Reviewed instruction bundle for a specific task.
- **Provenance:** Source, submitter, date, and approval evidence.
- **Archive:** Historical material not assumed current.
- **Sync:** Explicit, logged transfer; not implied by editing a file.
